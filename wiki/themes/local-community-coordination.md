# Local Community Coordination

## Overview

Coordinating underused local resources — objects, skills, time — among neighbors who don't yet trust each other. The recurring problem in this space is density and trust on a small scale: a sparse, city-wide map is worthless, so the system has to work on one street before it generalizes.

## Patterns Observed

Ideas in this theme tend to be feasible to build and easy to copy, so distinctiveness is consistently the weak score. Where defensibility exists, it comes from accumulated local inventory and trust rather than from the product. The common failure mode is underestimating the cold-start problem on the first block.

## Open Questions

- What is the cheapest way to bootstrap trust and density on a single street before any product exists?
- Does success on one street produce a repeatable playbook, or does every neighborhood have to be cold-started from scratch?
