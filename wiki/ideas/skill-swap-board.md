# Skill Swap Board

## Concept

A neighborhood board for trading skills instead of objects: someone who can fix a bike trades an hour for someone who can patch drywall. Same locality and trust problem as the tool library, with a different unit of exchange. Aimed at neighbors who want to help each other without money changing hands.

## History

- **2026-01-15** ([[summaries/brainstorm-2026-01-15-example]]) — status: parked — Surfaced as an adjacent surface to the tool library; parked until the tool side proves the reciprocity model.

## Viability Assessment

**Desirability / Feasibility / Distinctiveness: 2/2/1**

- **Desirability (2):** Appealing in principle; unclear how often skills actually match up on one street.
- **Feasibility (2):** Buildable, but matching and scheduling are harder than listing objects.
- **Distinctiveness (1):** Time-banking and skill-swap apps already exist; little here is novel on its own.

## Strengths

Reuses the tool library's trust and locality infrastructure, so it's cheap to add as a second surface once that foundation exists.

## Open Questions

1. Does it have any path to traction on its own, or only as an add-on to a working tool library?
2. How do you match supply and demand of skills densely enough on a single street?
