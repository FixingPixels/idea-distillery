# Writing Coach

These rules are distilled from the edits you actually make to drafts. Apply them while drafting and revising — not as a post-hoc cleanup pass but as in-session gates raised before the bad sentence ships. This file is the prose equivalent of `wiki/patterns/`: a blind spot earns a rule once it has recurred across **≥2 drafts**, and the highest-leverage gate sits first.

A useful way to organize blind spots once they appear: **effect over accuracy** (reaching for a vivid line or easy takeaway at the cost of what's true) and **distrust of the reader** (narrating, judging, and signposting instead of laying out evidence and letting the reader arrive on their own).
